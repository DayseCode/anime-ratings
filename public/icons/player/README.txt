Put your own SVG icons in THIS folder, with exactly these file names (lowercase):

  prev.svg              previous song
  play.svg              play
  pause.svg             pause
  next.svg              next song
  volume.svg            sound on
  mute.svg              sound off
  eye.svg               "Show video" button
  eye-off.svg           "Hide video" button
  fullscreen.svg        enter full screen
  fullscreen-exit.svg   leave full screen
  open.svg              open the link in a new tab
  close.svg             close the player

Tips
- Any icon you do not add yet simply keeps the old symbol.
- The SVG should have a viewBox, e.g. viewBox="0 0 24 24", and no background.
- The colours inside the SVG are used as they are. The player is dark,
  so use light colours (white / light grey / your accent colour).
